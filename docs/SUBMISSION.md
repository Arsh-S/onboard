# Onboard submission

## One sentence

Onboard is Muse recreated offline: a personal agent on your Mac that reads your screen as text, acts with its own cursor, and answers out loud, using PrismML Bonsai 2 and Argmax with WiFi off.

## 60 word blurb

Onboard is a personal agent on your Mac that works with WiFi off. It reads the app in front of you as text through the accessibility tree, so PrismML Bonsai 2 plans each click in under a second. Argmax WhisperKit hears you and TTSKit answers aloud. It searches your notes, asks before risky actions, and sends nothing to any cloud.

# Habitat Manual: Life Support

Ares Base, Habitat Module 1. Revision 7. Covers air, water and the CO2 scrubber on panel LS-2.

## CO2 scrubber fault light
The amber fault light means cabin CO2 is above 4.0 mmHg or bed airflow is low.
1. Read cabin CO2 on panel LS-2 and log it.
2. Check the bed inlet filter and clear any dust.
3. Press RESET on the scrubber and wait 10 minutes.
4. If still above 4.0, swap in a spare sorbent bed.
5. Recheck every 2 hours until below 4.0.
Above 6.0 mmHg: masks on, report to Mission Control.

## Safe CO2 partial pressure range
Nominal: 2.0 to 4.0 mmHg.
Caution: 4.0 to 6.0 mmHg. Follow the fault procedure and log every reading.
Action limit: 6.0 mmHg. Masks on and report at once.
CO2 margin is the action limit minus the current reading. Example: a reading of 4.1 leaves 1.9 mmHg of margin.
Headache, slow thinking and poor sleep can start above 5.0 mmHg.

## When to swap the sorbent bed
Swap the active bed when any one of these is true:
- Cabin CO2 stays above 4.0 mmHg for two readings taken 2 hours apart.
- The bed has run 30 sols (rated life).
- The bed color strip has turned from blue to grey along its full length.
A swap takes 15 minutes and one person. Wear gloves. Bag the used bed and label it with the sol.

## Sorbent margin
Each spare bed is rated for 30 sols.
Sorbent margin in days = spare beds remaining x 30.
Example: 4 spare beds = 120 days of margin.
Minimum allowed margin is 60 days. Below that, report to Mission Control.
The next cargo lander is due on Sol 300.

## Oxygen generation
The electrolysis unit makes 3.4 kg of oxygen per sol at full power.
Nominal cabin oxygen: 19.5 to 23.0 percent.
If oxygen falls below 19.5 percent, open reserve tank O2-B by a quarter turn and check the electrolysis breaker on panel P-1.

## Water recovery
The recycler returns 93 percent of waste water.
Change the particulate filter every 45 sols or when flow drops under 1.2 liters per hour.
Potable tank low mark: 180 liters. Below that, ration to 2.5 liters per person per sol.

## Cabin pressure and leaks
Nominal cabin pressure: 70 kPa. Alarm at 65 kPa.
For a slow leak, close the hatch between modules, then use the ultrasonic wand along the seals.
Patch kits are in locker L-4.

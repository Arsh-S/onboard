# Onboard demo video (hard limit: under 2 minutes, aim for 1:45)

Speakers ON (no headphones) so the mic records Onboard's voice.
Wait one beat after each request. If a take fails, repeat that line and keep going.

## 0. Open (18 s)  [settings window in front]
SAY: "This is Onboard. Cloud assistants that control your computer send your screen
and your voice to a data center. This one runs entirely on this Mac. Watch."

[hold fn]  "Turn the WiFi off."
(Onboard turns it off and says "WiFi is off." Point at the WiFi icon in the menu bar.)

SAY: "Everything from here on is offline. Bonsai 2 from PrismML does the thinking
through LM Studio. Argmax does the hearing, the speaking, and knows my voice."

## 1. Reminder (10 s)  [hold fn]
"Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow at 9."

## 2. It asks instead of guessing (8 s)  [hold fn]
"Remind me."
(It answers: "What should the reminder say, and when?")

## 3. It works the app in front with its own cursor (14 s)  [click the Maintenance log window, hold fn]
"Add a line that says pump A checked."
(Watch the teal cursor fly to the document, type the line, and check its own work.)

## 4. It rewrites what you select, in place (14 s)
DO: drag to select the line "hey can u send me the pump numbers asap thx".
[hold fn]  "Make this sound more professional."
(The sentence is replaced right in the document.)

## 5. Calendar (6 s)  [hold fn]
"Add lunch with Jared to my calendar Friday at noon."

## 6. Hands-free (15 s)  [no keys]
SAY first: "No keyboard at all."
"Onboard."   (wait for the pill)
"Remind me to submit the lab report."

## 7. Voice cancel (10 s)  [no keys]
"Onboard."   then   "What does this document say?"   then, while it works:   "Cancel."

## 8. Close (12 s)
SAY: "It only obeys my voice, and it checks its work before it answers.
On a plane, in a lab with no signal, or with files that can't leave the building,
it still works. Zero bytes to the cloud. That's Onboard."

## After recording
1. Turn WiFi back on: hold fn and say "Turn the WiFi on", or use the menu bar.
2. Check the video is under 2:00. If it is over, cut section 5.
3. Upload it, then post the submission.

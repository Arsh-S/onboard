# Onboard demo video (about 90 seconds)

Speakers ON (no headphones) so the mic records Onboard's voice.
Pause one beat after each request. If a take fails, repeat that line and move on.

## 0. Open (10 s)  [settings window in front]
SAY: "This is Onboard. It's an agent with its own cursor that runs my Mac by voice.
Everything, hearing, thinking and speaking, runs on this laptop. Zero bytes go to the cloud."

## 1. Reminder (10 s)  [hold fn]
"Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow at 9."

## 2. It asks instead of guessing (8 s)  [hold fn]
"Remind me."
(It answers: "What should the reminder say, and when?")

## 3. Calendar (8 s)  [hold fn]
"Add lunch with Bilal to my calendar Friday at noon."

## 4. Writing in an app (12 s)  [click the Maintenance log window, hold fn]
"Add a line that says pump A checked."
(Watch the cursor type it and check its own work.)

## 5. Answers from my files (10 s)  [hold fn]
"What do my notes say about filter changes?"

## 6. Hands-free (15 s)  [no keys]
"Onboard."   (wait for the pill)
"Remind me to submit the lab report."

## 7. Voice cancel (10 s)  [no keys]
"Onboard."   then   "What does this document say?"   then, while it works:   "Cancel."

## 8. Close (8 s)
SAY: "It only obeys my voice, it checks its work before it answers, and it works with the wifi off.
That's Onboard."

Stop recording: click the stop button in the menu bar (or Cmd+Ctrl+Esc).
